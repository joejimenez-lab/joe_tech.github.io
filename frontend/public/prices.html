Pricing questions

Can I choose the type of replacement display?

Yes. I offer budget, premium, and original Apple displays. Send your iPhone model and I’ll explain the options, confirm availability, and quote each choice so you can decide.

Can I get an estimate without bringing it in?

Yes. Send me the model and a description of the problem. I’ll let you know if I need to see the device first.

Is an older device worth repairing?

We can compare the repair cost with the device’s age and condition. Then you can decide whether it makes sense to fix it.
