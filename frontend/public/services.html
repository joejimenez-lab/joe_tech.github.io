Before arranging a repair

How long will it take?

It depends on the repair and whether I have the parts. Send your model and the issue so I can check availability and give you an estimate.

What if I don’t know what’s wrong?

Tell me what happens when you try to use it. Does it turn on? Does it charge? A photo or short video helps.

Can you look at water damage?

Contact me about an assessment. Repair and data recovery depend on how much damage there is.
